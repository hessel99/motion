Also remove the stray #6b8cff slash fill so the palette is navy, white, grey-blue and one accent.
- **02-kinetic-quote**: The phrase transitions are generic whole-layer fade-and-drift exits on default Easy Ease. At 2.17s the outgoing "Design is not" collides with the incoming "just w…", and the line looks shifted off its centred position, so the hand-off reads as a muddle rather than a designed transition. The type system is also inconsistent: "Design is" is set at 120px, then 100px, and the baselines jump between beats. / Choreograph the hand-offs per word or letter, with a staggered Expo-In exit fully clearing before or masking the next phrase's entry. Lock all phrases to one shared centre and baseline grid. Give the "how it works" landing a real spring overshoot instead of the 108→104 scale nudge. Raise the attribution's contrast and give it a slight rise, so the final hold reads as a deliberate payoff.
- **03-toggle-micro**: Mid-toggle reads poorly: at 0.52s the knob is stretched to 126/88, a flat egg rather than a subtle squash, while the 0.4–0.8s opacity crossfade of green over grey produces a muddy grey-green track. On top of that, the checkmark's trim keys don't render, so it only fades in instead of drawing on. / Reduce the stretch to about 112/92 and switch the knob travel to a spring or Back-out ease so the overshoot has life. Replace the opacity crossfade with a direct fill-color key from #d1d1d6 to #34c759, and fix the Check path so its trimEnd draw-on actually renders, with a small scale pop at the end.
