## Judge critique

- **01-logo-reveal**: The piece is thin: once drawn, the mark never reacts again, and the wordmark slides in from a hard clip edge in the gap beside the ring rather than from behind it, so the 'slides out from behind' beat reads as a plain wipe; the 40px dim #8fa3bf tagline is also too small and low-contrast to hold its tier in the lockup. / Anchor the wordmark clip to the ring's right edge, overlapping it so NOVA emerges from under the ring, and have the slash flick or the ring settle as the word launches. Then scale the tagline to about 56–64px with a brighter tint and give it a short stagger, so the end hold reads as a deliberate, balanced lockup.
- **02-kinetic-quote**: The comp runs 10s instead of the briefed 8s, and the attribution only appears at 8.0s; within the requested duration it never lands. Each phrase is the same generic move (word fade-in, hold, 40px drift-up fade-out), so the beats stack in sequence instead of transitioning into each other, and the hero gets no lead-in. / Retime to 8s and land the attribution by about 6.8s. Build real hand-offs between phrases, e.g. 'Design is' stays as an anchor and slides up while 'not just…' wipes out and 'how it works.' punches in beneath it with scale and overshoot. Also give the hero more side margin than the current ~40px.
- **03-toggle-micro**: The interaction is technically complete but thin: the knob is a flat white disc with no drop shadow or press/anticipation, and the 4px overshoot on a 78px throw is barely perceptible, so it reads as a generic tween rather than a tactile iOS toggle. / Add a press beat before the toggle (knob widens ~0.3–0.4s), give the knob a soft shadow, and drive the slide with a real spring (~8–10px overshoot settling near 1.1s), with the checkmark drawing after the knob lands and a small scale pop.
- **04-explainer-3beat**: Beat 2 barely exists: the notes shrink to nothing at center in under a second, the shield pops in while they vanish, and then nothing moves from 5.1s to 7.9s. The middle third reads as dead air rather than a 'sucked into the vault' handover. The whole pile is also tiny in a 1920 frame (unreadable 28px labels), and the headline jumps from a left-offset partial build to centered. / Rebuild beat 2 as a continuous 4s action. Bring the shield in first (around 3s), then pull the notes into it along curved paths with staggered Cubic In anticipation. Have the shield react to each impact with a squash and pulse. Carry that energy into the glow at about 7.5s with no hold. Scale the notes and shield up to about 2.5x, and lock the headline's anchor so it builds centered.
- **05-bar-chart**: All motion is finished by 2.6s, leaving a 3.4s near-static hold (only a barely perceptible 4% scale creep on '27'). The entrance also feels rushed: bars at 0.15s stagger with 0.7s Quart Out, and labels fade in at their final height before their bars arrive, so they float over empty space for a moment. / Spread the build across about 4s: give each bar roughly 0.25s stagger and a slightly longer grow. Have each label count up from 0 to its value while riding the bar's top edge. Then hold Q4's pop as a deliberate final beat, e.g. a soft accent glow or tick-up on the '27' at about 3.5s, before a clean 1.5s end hold.
- **06-loop-bg**: The motion is too thin for a stage-screen background. Every orb follows one sine orbit at the same 6s period, so they drift in visible lockstep. The dot grid is a single path that just scales 4% and fades in unison, so there's no per-dot pulse, no wave, and no secondary motion. The orbs also never change scale, opacity or hue. / Rebuild the dot grid as per-dot layers (or a repeater) whose opacity pulses with a phase offset by distance from centre, using integer harmonics of 2π/6 so it still loops. Then give the orbs layered motion that also loops: 2x/3x harmonic drift, a slow scale breathe and an opacity breathe. Rebalance the orbs too, so the violet/fuchsia mass isn't stacked top-left-centre and the right and bottom-left edges carry colour.
- **07-product-promo**: After a strong slam at 3.5s the piece goes dead: nothing moves from ~4.1s to 6s and again from 6.6s to 8.2s (30% visual activity). The feature-word 'energetic transitions' are just hard-cut entrances with a 0.1s fade-out, so the 120 BPM energy collapses into static holds. / Fill the 4–6s gap on the beat grid: add beat-synced pulses (a subtle title scale tick, a small ring echo or orange glow every 0.5s), bring 'Available now' in earlier at 5s, and replace the word fades with directional whip/slice exits that hand off into the next word's entrance.
- **09-playful-bouncy**: The candy drop is one copy-pasted keyframe template (same 0.42s fall, same 130/74 squash, same three-step settle, same 4° sine wiggle) with no jostling between shapes. The letters squash to 125% wide, so they visibly collide mid-hop (o2 over o1 and p at ~2.27s, B over the pink circle at ~1.82s). They also pop in on an 8-frame opacity fade from below instead of landing from above. / Rebuild the letter hop as a true trampoline cycle: start each letter above frame, stretch it on the fall, squash it on contact, and rebound on a spring. Keep squash width below the letter spacing, or offset the gaps, so neighbours never overlap. Then make the candies jostle: have each landing nudge its nearest neighbour with a small position/rotation spring, and vary drop durations. Fill the dead 2.6–3s gap by starting the idle bob (scale + y, not just 4° rotation) as soon as the '!' lands. Move the pale-pink circle away from the '!'.
- **10-corporate-smooth**: The 'soft mask' reveal isn't real: the mask groups are empty (white at 0 alpha, no matte), so name and title just fade and drift up 30-50px over the panel. The panel only nudges 40px, and the 0.2-0.4s Quart In exits snap out faster than the calm, premium entrance. / Build true track-matte reveals clipped to the panel (text rises from below a hard edge as the panel wipes open from the accent line), and stretch the exits to about 0.6-0.8s with Quart In Out, staggered in reverse order (title, name, panel, line) so the out reads as smooth as the in.
- **12-hand-drawn**: The 'hand-drawn' feel never arrives: the only boil is a 1.4px stepped position jitter that reads as nothing at 1920px, the strokes and the semicircle loaf are clean geometric curves, and the heavy default-looking serif wordmark feels corporate rather than friendly, so after the 2.8s draw-on the piece is a static, mechanical lockup for 3+ seconds. / Add a real 12fps boil by cycling 2-3 hand-jittered path keyframes (or a posterized wiggle on path vertices/stroke width of ~4-6px) on the wheat, loaf and blob outlines, redraw the loaf with irregular, uneven contours, and give the wordmark a rounder hand-lettered face with a soft per-letter bounce/rotation settle; also rebalance the cluster so the sage blob and the empty lower-right feel intentional.
- **15-minimal-luxury**: After the letters settle at ~3.7s, the last 3.2s is a near-static hold: only a 3% group scale moves, and 'EAU DE PARFUM' snaps in with a 0.6s fade (which fails the slow brief). It is also set so small (26px) and so tight under the line (~35px) that it reads as an afterthought rather than a designed second beat. / Make the descriptor a real beat. Enlarge it to ~34-38px with wide tracking (~0.4em), give it ~70-90px of air below the line, and fade it over 1.2-1.5s with its own slight tracking settle. Then fill the hold with one restrained detail, such as a slow soft glint travelling along the gold line or across the name around 4.5-6s, so the end feels finished rather than parked.
- **17-music-logo-sting**: The rhythm doesn't sit on the 120 BPM grid. The dot's beat pulses peak at x.62s and settle at x.8s instead of hitting on the beat, the ring trim finishes at 3.9s rather than 4.0s, the wordmark letters land around 4.65–4.85s, and the ripple hits at 4.9s and 6.9s. Only the 4.0s dot slam is on time, so the sting looks loosely timed to the music rather than synced, and the build is a tiny mark slowly tracing a thin ring in an empty, left-weighted frame with little sense of tension. / Put every accent on a beat. Make each dot pulse peak exactly on x.0/x.5s (attack from about 2 frames before), have the ring trim close at 4.0s together with the dot, land the wordmark's last character on 4.5s, and fire ripples at 4.5s and 5.5s or 6.0s. Fix the ripple's 4.9→6s segment so it resets invisibly instead of shrinking back while fading in, and give the build stronger escalation (ring thickening or brightening, faster pulses in the last bar) so the drop pays off.
