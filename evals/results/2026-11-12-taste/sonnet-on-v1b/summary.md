## Judge critique

- **01-logo-reveal**: The wordmark doesn't come from behind the mark. Its clip edge sits at x≈834, in empty space about 34px right of the circle (which ends at 800), so NOVA wipes on from an invisible line. At 1.82s that edge also leaves a stray vertical sliver beside the 'N'. Beyond that, the piece is only two stroke trims, one slide and one fade, with no secondary motion or settle. The tagline is a tiny 34px under a 150px wordmark, and the slash carries an unused leftover fill (#6b8cff) that inflates the palette. / Move the wordmark mask's left edge to the circle's center (x≈720) and put the mask layer above the circle in draw order, so NOVA visibly emerges from under the mark. Then add a small settle: a slight mark scale-pop when the slash lands, and a gentle 1.5s hold-in on the full lockup. Bump the tagline to about 44–48px with a short upward drift, and remove the stray slash fill.
- **02-kinetic-quote**: Motion is thin and repetitive: every phrase uses the same by-word Expo Out fade-in, the payoff 'how it works.' simply appears and stacks under a static 'Design is', and from ~5.8s to 8s nothing moves except a small attribution fade. With only 19% visual activity, it reads as a slideshow, not kinetic type. / Make the emphasis beat a real transition. Carry the first 'Design is' (150px) into the second one (110px, moved up) instead of re-fading it in. Drive 'how it works.' per letter with scale/overshoot and a punchy stagger, then add a subtle secondary move (slight scale-up or tracking drift) through the end hold so the final frame isn't dead.
- **03-toggle-micro**: The track crossfade interpolates grey→green in RGB and passes through a muddy, desaturated mint around 0.55s, right when the knob is at full stretch, so the key moment of the toggle looks washed out; the flat, shadowless knob also reads less iOS-like and more like clip art. / Crossfade a separate green track layer by opacity (or interpolate in a perceptual space) so saturation never dips, and give the knob a soft drop shadow, e.g. 0,3px blur 8 at 15-20% black, so it reads as a raised physical knob sliding over the track.
- **04-explainer-3beat**: Beat 1 barely reads as anxious: the only jitter is a ±4–5° sine rotation on a tight, undersized cluster (~600px wide on a 1920 frame) with no positional shake. The 0.6s Expo In suck-in is followed by a mostly idle shield (4.8–6s), and the comp sits in a dead 3.5s hold after the headline settles at 8.7s, so the rhythm is front-loaded and the ending drags. / Make beat 1 genuinely nervous and fill the frame: spread the notes wider and larger, and add per-note position wiggle (2–4px at varied frequencies) that escalates before the suck-in. Then redistribute timing so the glow, check and headline build continuously from ~5s with overlapping cues, and end with a subtle living hold (glow pulse or shield breathe) instead of a frozen last 3.5s.
- **05-bar-chart**: The bar sequence is strictly serial: each bar waits for the previous one to finish, all on the same Expo Out, so the build reads as a mechanical metronome rather than a cascade. The values only fade in instead of counting up, and Q4 is drawn at 555px where its data says 540px (the others scale at 20px per $M), which distorts the very bar being highlighted. / Overlap the bar entrances by about 0.3s and drive each label with a count-up tied to its bar's growth. Fix Q4's height to 540px and start its accent color from the first frame of its growth, so the extra pop (overshoot plus color) lands as one deliberate hero beat.
- **06-loop-bg**: Every blob runs one full 6s circular orbit at the same frequency with 60-100px radii, so the field reads as a single wobbling mass rather than calm drift. The pulsing dot grid is so faint behind the large overlapping blobs that it barely registers, and the centre turns into a muddy blue-purple smear with no clear areas of light and dark. / Vary the motion while keeping the loop: give blobs different whole-number frequencies (1x, 2x, 3x of 2π/6) at smaller amplitudes on independent x/y axes. Then raise the dot grid's contrast and add a slow radial falloff to its pulse, so the grid and the larger, more separated blobs read as distinct layers.
- **07-product-promo**: The comp runs 8s instead of the briefed 10s. Every feature beat is offset by 0.12s from the 0.5s/120 BPM grid, and the 2.3s 'PULSE 2' hold (3.2–5.5s) is a near-static drift. The piece feels clipped up front and then dead in the middle instead of cut to a beat. / Retime to 10s on a strict 0.5s grid with markers. Land each word and wipe exactly on the beat, give the words real exit moves instead of 0.05s fades, and use the extra time for beat-synced accents on the name hold (rings, a scale bump every 0.5s). Pulse the pill at 2Hz (120 BPM) rather than 1Hz.
- **09-playful-bouncy**: The core gags in the brief are missing. 'Squash & stretch' is one pre-stretched scale that springs back to 100, with no squash on landing. The shapes never jostle each other; each one drops into its own isolated slot. The letters rise once from below the frame on a single spring instead of hopping like they're on a trampoline. The result is closer to 'things spring into place' than elastic, joyful play. / Rebuild each landing as a real impact: fall stretched, squash to about [125,75] on contact, then a damped rebound. Have neighbouring shapes nudge each other on impact. Make each letter launch up, squash on a shared baseline and bounce 2-3 times, staggered. Also move the pink star out from under the 'B' and centre the wordmark in the gap between the top and bottom shape rows.
- **10-corporate-smooth**: The name and title collide on entry: Title starts 0.2s later but travels faster on Quart Out (0.6s) than Name on Cubic In Out (0.9s), so it passes through the name. At 1.63s the two lines are visibly stacked on top of each other, which breaks the calm, premium read; a similar crossing happens on exit (≈1.7s of text-on-text overlap in total). / Give Name and Title the same ease and duration (e.g. 0.9s Cubic/Quint In Out), with Title offset 0.15–0.2s behind Name on the way in and leading on the way out. Have each line rise only from just below its own baseline inside the mask instead of from y=175/190, so they never cross. Stretch the exit to about 0.9s so it matches the length of the entrance.
- **12-hand-drawn**: The hand-made promise breaks down in two places. The 'boil' is only a rigid 2px/0.7° stepped jitter of whole groups, so the lines never wobble. The name is set in stock Inter 600, which reads corporate. It is also dropped straight across blob1, blob3 and the loaf's bottom edge, so the title collides with the illustration and the tiny tagline gets lost. / Make the type and boil hand-made and give the name its own space. Swap Inter for a rounded or hand-lettered face, add true 12fps line boil (3–4 alternating path keys or per-vertex stepped noise on each stroke), and slowly morph the blob outlines. Then move the lockup below the drawings with clear margin so the wheat, loaf and name read as one composed logo.
- **15-minimal-luxury**: The piece is restrained but thin: a line, five letter fades and a subtitle. The comp runs 8s against the 7s brief. The 30px #b89d68 'EAU DE PARFUM' is so small and dim that it barely registers on a phone-sized story frame, and the line sits apart from the wordmark instead of resolving into it. / Trim the comp to 7s and give the ending a resolve. Fade the line's lower end into the name, add a slow, low-opacity champagne sheen across ÉCLAT after the tracking settles, and enlarge and brighten the subtitle (about 38–42px, closer to #c9ad76). Also remove the stray #6b8cff default fill on the line path and the 3px sine bob on the subtitle, which works against 'nothing should move'.
- **17-music-logo-sting**: After the drop, the main moves land off the 120 BPM grid: the mark settles at 5.3s, the wordmark at 5.6s and the ring kick at 6.05s, so the music sync falls apart right where it should feel tightest; the wordmark also slides through the ring (visible at 5.1s), which reads as a collision rather than a reveal. / Retime the lockup to the beat grid: move the mark from 4.5s to 5.0s, slide the wordmark in from 5.0s and land it at 5.5s together with the wave2/dot hit, and start it from behind a mask at the ring's right edge so it never crosses the mark. Then give the end hold a final small settle on 6.5s.
