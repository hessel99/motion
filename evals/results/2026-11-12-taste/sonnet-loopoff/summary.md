## Judge critique

- **01-logo-reveal**: The piece is a strictly serial chain of single-property moves (ring trim, slash trim, wordmark slide+fade, tagline nudge+fade) with no overlap, settle, or secondary motion, so it reads as a correct template rather than a crafted reveal. The 38px muted tagline is undersized and low-contrast against a 150px w800 wordmark, which weakens the final lockup. / Overlap and enrich the handoffs: start the wordmark slide while the slash is still finishing. Give the mark a small scale/rotation settle or an accent pulse as the slash lands. Add a real clip mask so the wordmark emerges from the ring's edge instead of relying on the backing occluder plus an opacity fade. Enlarge the tagline (about 48–54px, brighter) and tighten its baseline to the wordmark.
- **02-kinetic-quote**: The phrases don't transition into each other: each one holds static for about 1.8s, then exits with a quick Cubic In slide-up and fade while the next one types on top of it (the 'ju' collides with the fading 'Design is not' around 2.1s), so it plays as three separate slides with dead holds, and the 150px 'how it works.' runs to within about 45px of the frame edges, outside a safe margin. / Choreograph real handoffs: let the shared word 'Design' carry from p1 into p3a, drive the exits per word or letter with a stagger so outgoing and incoming text interleave without overlapping, shorten the static holds with subtle drift or scale, and size 'how it works.' to about 130px so it keeps a safe margin while still dominating.
- **03-toggle-micro**: The knob is a flat white disc with no drop shadow, no press or anticipation beat, and no depth, so the interaction reads as a vector diagram rather than an iOS-style control, even though the squash, overshoot and check timing are correct. / Give the knob a soft drop shadow that tightens during the stretch, and add a short press squash (about 0.33-0.4s, knob widening slightly before it launches) so the toggle feels tactile; also delay the checkmark to about 0.8s, after the overshoot peaks, so it lands on a settling knob.
- **04-explainer-3beat**: The beat 2→3 handover stalls: after the last note lands (6.2s) and the shield settles (6.5s), there is about 1.1s of dead hold, and then the keyhole fades out while the check trims on as a separate swap. The payoff reads as a reset rather than a continuation, and the beat-1 'anxiety' is rotation-only wiggle, so the pile feels tilted rather than nervous. / Start the glow on the final note impact at about 6.2s, then morph or scale-pop the keyhole directly into the check with a small shield squash, so absorption, glow and check form one continuous chain. Also add a low-amplitude position wiggle (with a scale tremble) to the notes so beat 1 actually reads as jittery.
- **05-bar-chart**: The piece is correct but thin: motion ends at 3.47s and then holds for 2.5s, the value labels only fade and rise instead of counting up, and the Q4 'pop' is just an 8% overshoot plus a color change, so the climax has little impact and over 40% of the runtime is static. / Make the labels count up from 0 in sync with each bar's growth, and give Q4 a real hero beat around 3.5–4.5s (for example a scale punch on the '27' label, a subtle glow or shadow on the bar, and a slight dim of Q1–Q3). This fills the dead hold and makes the highlight read as the payoff.
- **06-loop-bg**: The piece is thin. Each orb only translates on one small circular sine path, with no scale, opacity or hue breathing. The 'dot grid' is 9 row strips pulsing as whole lines rather than individual dots, so the pulse reads as horizontal banding. The weight also clusters at the top and bottom edges: Violet and Indigo are cropped at the top, Cyan is half off the bottom, and the dark center is left mostly empty. / Add slow loop-safe secondary motion to each orb, such as scale 0.92–1.08 and opacity breathing at 2π·t/6 with offset phases. Rebuild the grid as individual dots, or columns × rows, with a radial or diagonal phase offset so the pulse ripples across the field. Pull the orbs inward so they overlap through the center and blend into purple-teal mixes instead of hugging the frame edges.
- **07-product-promo**: The feature section is one identical orange-bar wipe repeated four times, drawn above the words at a 1s/60 BPM cadence with keys 0.05s off the 0.5s grid. Full-frame orange floods half of the opening frames, the words get only about 0.7s of clean read each, the comp is 8s instead of the briefed 10s, and the 'Available now' label sits high and undersized in its pill. / Rebuild the first 4s on a strict 0.5s grid: cut each word in on the downbeat and use thinner or angled orange slashes (or a fast clip mask) that reveal the word instead of covering it. Then extend to 10s so PULSE 2 and the pill get a real hold, and center and enlarge the pill label (about 64–72px, vertically centered in a taller pill).
- **09-playful-bouncy**: Every shape and letter runs the same copy-pasted drop/squash/bounce, offset only by a stagger, so it feels mechanical: the shapes never jostle each other as briefed, the letters fade in mid-air at y=300 over the shape row instead of launching off a trampoline, and the end wiggle is a faint rotation-only sine. / Give each element its own bounce height, timing and squash, and add contact beats: shapes nudge their neighbours on landing, letters spring up from below the baseline with a real overshooting spring (not a fade-in), each landing kicks the next letter, and the idle loop becomes a squash/bob plus rotation.
- **10-corporate-smooth**: The 'mask' groups are empty white a0 containers that clip nothing: name and title just slide up 64px/48px while fading, so the text visibly rises from below the panel's bottom edge (f49), the panel drifts misaligned left of the accent line during entry and exit (f33, f164), and the exit sends the text down while the panel moves left, so the build reads as a generic fade-slide, not a premium masked reveal. / Turn the name and title groups into real clip masks bounded by the panel's inner rect, and reduce the text travel to about 30px. Anchor the panel's slide to the accent line's left edge so their left edges stay locked, and mirror the entry for the exit (text drops behind the mask, then the panel retracts, then the line retracts toward its origin) with an even stagger of about 0.15s.
- **12-hand-drawn**: The 'hand-drawn' quality is only simulated: the stalk and loaf are clean, uniform 8px vector strokes, and the boil is a whole-layer 12fps position/rotation jitter rather than wobbling line shapes, so the piece reads as tidy clip-art shaking slightly; the same jitter on the wordmark, plus the 'Co' sitting half on the green blob, makes the name feel unstable and cluttered. / Build real boil by cycling 3 slightly different hand-drawn path keyframes (or a per-vertex wiggle) at 12fps with tapered, variable-width strokes, remove the jitter from the name, and rebalance the layout so the wordmark sits on clean background and the stalk, loaf and blobs frame it as one grouped lockup.
- **15-minimal-luxury**: Playfair Display failed to load, so 'ÉCLAT' renders in a generic sans fallback. That throws away the wide-tracked serif the whole luxury read depends on, and the requested tracking-tighten was swapped for a 106→100 scale that barely registers. / Embed or load the serif so it actually renders, and animate letter-spacing (e.g. ~+400 → +250 over 2s→6s, Sine Out) instead of scale. Then give the subtitle more size and contrast and tie the line's end into the name (e.g. line finishing as the first letter lands) so the three elements read as one composed lockup.
- **17-music-logo-sting**: After the 4.0 s drop the motion stops following the music: the slide (4.5→5.1 s), the wordmark fade (4.7→5.2 s) and the dot peaks at x.15/x.65 all land off the 0.5 s grid, so the secondary hits read as drift instead of punctuation. The wordmark gets only a plain opacity fade, with no motion of its own. / Put every landing on the beat grid: slide 4.5→5.0, wordmark arriving at 5.0 with a short x-offset and scale settle, and shock/ring/dot hits peaking exactly at 5.5 and 6.0. Then center the final lockup as a group; it currently sits about 100 px right of center.
