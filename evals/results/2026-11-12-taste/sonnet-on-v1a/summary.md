## Judge critique

- **01-logo-reveal**: The piece is a minimal checklist build: each element gets one eased move and stops, with no secondary motion, overlap or settle. The mark sits inert and off-center for 1.3s while it draws, and the 1.2–1.3s gap plus the late tagline make the rhythm feel stepwise rather than choreographed. / Choreograph the hand-offs. Start the mark centered and have it glide left (Expo Out) as NOVA pushes out from behind its edge, overlapping the slash finish with the wordmark start. Give the mark a small scale/rotation settle on the slash hit, and bring the tagline in with a subtle tracking or per-word stagger. The lockup then resolves as one connected move into the hold.
- **02-kinetic-quote**: The middle of the piece goes static and generic: 'just what it looks like.' sits as a dead grey block for about 2s (2.9–4.4s), and every phrase exit is the same Expo-In fade-and-drift-up, so the 'transitions' are really crossfades. Meanwhile the hero reveal stutters: at 5.83s 'how' sits isolated at the far left while 'it' is a tiny red speck, which reads as a layout glitch rather than a build. / Make the phrases hand off to each other: carry 'Design is not' into 'Design is' as one continuous layer (strike or knock out 'not'), give p2 a per-word exit, and pace the 'how it works.' build so it stays visually centred, either grown from its centre or with a tight word stagger and slight overshoot. That removes the dead hold and gives the emphasis a real payoff moment.
- **03-toggle-micro**: The track's RGB fill interpolation from #c7c7cc to #34c759 passes through a muddy sage grey-green (visible at 0.55s), and the knob is a flat white disc with no drop shadow or edge. At the most visible moment it reads as a generic vector mock rather than a crisp iOS switch. / Add a soft, offset drop shadow to the knob, scaled with its squash. Drive the color change as a true crossfade by putting a green track layer over the grey one and fading its opacity (or interpolate in a perceptual color space), so the midpoint stays clean and bright. A 1-2 frame press-in scale dip before 0.4s would add the anticipation the move currently lacks.
- **04-explainer-3beat**: The middle beat is rushed and then stalls: all six notes are sucked in within about 1.5s (3.6–5.1s) and fade out before they visibly reach the shield, then from 5.8s to 7.3s nothing moves, so the 'sucked into the vault' payoff is lost and beat 3 starts from a near-hard cut (the wash snaps to full within frames). / Rebalance beat 2 to about 3–4s: give the notes curved, staggered paths that shrink into the shield's mouth with a scale squash and a shield gulp/pulse on each impact, and start the shield's move to center and the wash bloom while the last notes are still arriving. That removes the dead hold and creates a true hand-off into beat 3.
- **05-bar-chart**: All motion ends by 3.3s, leaving a 2.7s dead hold (44% of the comp). The Q4 'pop' is undercut by a 0.5s grey-to-orange fill crossfade that passes through a muddy mauve (visible at 2.73s), so the highlight lands as a smear instead of a hit. / Re-pace and sharpen the payoff. Spread the bar stagger across roughly 1.2–3.8s. Grow Q4 directly in the accent color, or snap it to accent on the frame it lands, and pair that with the 104% overshoot plus a count-up on the value labels. Leave a ~1.2s end hold.
- **06-loop-bg**: Every orb and both dot layers run the same 3s out-and-back on identical Sine In Out keys, so the whole frame breathes in lockstep. It reads as one mechanical pulse instead of independent ambient drift, and the dot 'pulse' is just two flat layers crossfading, barely visible against the orbs. / Desynchronise the motion: give each orb its own looping path with 3-4 offset keys or phase-shifted sine expressions over periods that divide 6s (6/3/2/1.5s), varying amplitude and adding slight opacity breathing. Rebuild the dot grid as a field of dots with a radial or diagonal wave of opacity/scale travelling across it, so the pulse has direction and the loop stays seamless.
- **07-product-promo**: After a strong first four seconds (beat-cut wipes, slam with shake, flash and double shockwave) the energy collapses: PULSE 2 sits frozen from 4.3s to 6.5s, and everything is still from 7.47s to 10s. That is about 4.7s of dead, unsynced holds in a piece briefed as fast and cut to 120 BPM. / Keep the back half on the 0.5s grid: add a subtle beat pulse on the name/bar (scale 100→102, glow or bar flicker every 0.5s), bring the CTA in earlier around 5.5–6s, and give the pill a beat-timed sheen or arrow nudge loop. Scale the pill and its text up roughly 1.4×, because 'Available now' currently reads as an afterthought under the 300px title.
- **09-playful-bouncy**: The letters launch upward along a path that runs straight through the pink circle and yellow star (the B collides with the circle around 2.27s, the ! with the star around 3.18s). The 'jostle' is just one synchronized rotation keyframe on every shape at 1.8s, so the shapes never react to each other or to the landing letters. The final wordmark sits on an uneven baseline with uneven spacing. / Clear a dedicated lane for the wordmark, or have the shapes visibly get knocked aside as each letter lands, with an offset nudge and spring per impact. Replace the no-op 100→100 'springs' and the Quad bounces with real overshooting springs, then lock the letters to a single baseline with optical kerning before the idle wiggle starts.
- **10-corporate-smooth**: The build is the bare minimum: one line scale, one 60px panel drift with a fade, and two text slides, all on the same Cubic In Out. The exit collapses everything within ~0.8s and leaves only a 0.27s clear tail. It reads as competent template motion, not premium design; the 780px panel also sits half-empty to the right of short text. / Give the motion a choreographed hierarchy. Let the accent line lead with a longer custom ease-out (e.g. 0.2–1.6s) and an end-cap or light glint. Trail the panel wipe from the line's position rather than fading in place. Stagger the exit in reverse order (title, name, panel, line) so it finishes by ~5.4s. Tighten the panel width to the text length plus padding.
- **12-hand-drawn**: The stem and loaf-score paths have a leftover default blue fill (#6b8cff) that flashes as a visible blue sliver during the stem draw-on (0.5–1.1s). The title is set in clean Inter Bold with no boil, so the name reads corporate and mechanical against the hand-drawn art. The 12fps boil is only a transform jitter on whole layers, not a wobble in the line itself. / Set the stem and score fills to none. Swap the title for a rounded hand-lettered or brush face and give it the same stepped 12fps jitter, ideally as a per-character draw-on or wobble. Add 2–3 stepped path-key or turbulent-displace variants to the strokes so the lines themselves boil instead of just shifting position.
- **15-minimal-luxury**: The gold line's Quart Out trim does most of its drawing in the first ~0.8s, so it lunges instead of slowly drawing, and the 0.85–1.0 opacity shimmer makes it read as dim brown rather than champagne gold. The lockup also sits high (line top ~160px, subline ~1175px) with a large empty lower half and a large gap between line and name, so the negative space looks unbalanced rather than designed. / Redraw the line with a Sine In Out (or linear-into-ease-out) trim over ~3s at full gold opacity, and vertically center the whole line + ÉCLAT + hairline + subline group around y≈960 with a tighter line-to-name gap. While doing that, enlarge the subline to ~32–36px and brighten it so 'EAU DE PARFUM' stays legible in story format.
- **17-music-logo-sting**: The secondary hits drift off the 120 BPM grid: the shock fades at 4.7s, the wordmark settles at 4.95s and the ring and wordmark bump at 6.12s, so after a clean 4.0s drop the sting stops feeling locked to the music. The build pulses resolve at x.3/x.8s too, and the lockup sits slightly right of center with the wordmark crammed against the ring while the shock ring cuts through the text at 4.4s. / Re-time every post-drop accent to beat frames (4.5s, 5.0s, 5.5s, 6.0s) with one clear hit per chosen beat, and keep the shock at 0 opacity until the drop instead of ramping it up from 0s so the 4.0s burst is a reveal. Then widen the ring-to-wordmark gap and center the full lockup on 960.
