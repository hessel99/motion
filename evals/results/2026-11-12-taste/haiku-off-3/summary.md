## Judge critique

- **01-logo-reveal**: The piece is thin: five moving segments with one move per layer and no secondary motion, settle, or interaction between mark and wordmark (the slash just scales up from the ring's center, and the tagline is a stock fade-up), so it reads as a competent template rather than a crafted reveal. / Make the mark assemble with intent: sweep the slash in along its diagonal so it lands as the ring's trim closes, with a small overshoot or settle. Then have the wordmark's slide visibly start from that impact, with a slight stagger on the letters. Also enlarge or brighten the tagline: at 40px #9fb3cc it is close to illegible against the 150px wordmark.
- **02-kinetic-quote**: Every beat uses the same word fade-in plus Easy Ease drift-up-and-fade exit, so the payoff 'how it works.' arrives with no more energy than the setup lines. Each incoming phrase also starts while the previous one is still half-faded in the same spot (see 3.63s), which reads as a muddy ghosted overlap rather than a designed transition. / Make the phrase handoffs real choreography: clear or push the outgoing words before the next ones land, or have 'Design is' visibly replace 'what it looks like'. Then give 'how it works.' its own stronger entrance (per-letter scale/overshoot or slam-in with a settle and a subtle color or weight pulse) so the emphasis beat carries the climax and fills the static final 2 seconds.
- **03-toggle-micro**: The interaction lands technically but feels thin: everything resolves in about 0.4s (0.4–0.8s), followed by a 1.2s dead hold. There is no press/anticipation beat, no knob drop shadow, and no secondary settle, and a 120px toggle floats tiny in an empty 800x600 frame, so it reads as a spec demo rather than a crafted micro-interaction. / Add a press-in anticipation at ~0.3–0.4s (knob widens slightly, track dims) and a soft iOS knob shadow. Sync the squash to the overshoot peak, delay the checkmark until the knob lands. Scale the toggle up to ~2.5x so the squash, stretch and check detail actually read, and extend the settle to ~1.1s so the motion fills the brief's window.
- **04-explainer-3beat**: The sticky-note pile is tiny (notes ~35-110px wide in a 1920x1080 frame) and huddled at center for the first 4s, so beat 1 reads as a small static clump. The sine-expression jitter (6px/7°) is barely perceptible at that scale, and the 'anxious mess' never fills the frame or creates tension; the shield glow is a faint amber smudge and the check badge is undersized. / Scale the notes up 2-3x and scatter them across the frame with varied rotations, then hold the shield closed for the suck-in. Make the notes visibly overlap the shield as they shrink into the keyhole, with a squash/pulse on each impact. That turns beat 1→2 into a real handoff with payoff instead of notes vanishing in the air while the shield pops in beside them.
- **05-bar-chart**: All motion ends by 2.9s, leaving a 3.1s static hold over half the comp; the brief's requested count-up labels are plain fades, and the Q4 'pop' (a 104/102 scale nudge) barely registers, so the hero beat lands no harder than the other bars. / Stretch the build across roughly 4.5s with wider bar staggers. Have the value labels count up from 0 in sync with each bar's growth. Then give Q4 a distinct beat: a longer, overshooting spring growth, a label scale-pop, and a subtle dim of Q1-Q3, held about 1s before the end.
- **06-loop-bg**: The orbs only translate on short 6s/3s sine Lissajous paths. Violet mid and teal small reach about 210-250px/s, which reads more 'bobbing' than 'drifting' for a stage background. Nothing breathes in scale or opacity, so the piece feels mechanical. The 6px dots at 10-50% alpha in an inset 8x5 grid are nearly invisible and stop short of the frame edges like a boxed overlay. / Cut orb amplitudes or angular speeds so peak drift stays under about 80px/s, keeping integer multiples of 2π/6 so the loop holds. Add a slow loop-safe scale/opacity breath on each orb with offset phases. Extend the dot grid edge-to-edge with denser spacing and slightly higher peak alpha so the diagonal pulse wave actually reads on a large screen.
- **07-product-promo**: PULSE 2 has no opacity key before 2.5s, so it sits on screen from frame 0 at 150% scale, overflowing the frame and colliding with every feature word; this destroys the feature-flash beats and steals the slam's reveal. The rest of the timing is also off: 'Spatial audio' gets ~0.4s, 'Pure silence' lingers 1.36s, the ring hard-cuts off on a hold instead of fading, and all motion ends at 4s, leaving a dead 6s hold. / Key PULSE 2 (and the CTA) to opacity 0 until 2.5s, then re-space the whole sequence across the 10s on the 0.5s grid. Give each word an equal 1-beat flash with an orange wipe between every pair, land the slam around 4–5s with an Expo Out ring that fades out, bring the pill in on the next downbeat, and add a subtle pulse or glow loop on the end card so the final hold stays alive.
- **09-playful-bouncy**: Every candy and every letter runs the same copy-pasted keyframe recipe (identical drop/squash/jostle curves, just offset by 0.1s or 0.12s), so the piece feels mechanical, not elastic. The 'jostle' is a tiny horizontal nudge with no contact between shapes. The letters launch up from y=860 through the candy cluster, which causes about 2.3s of messy overlaps (p and ! cross the violet star and pink square at 3.18s). The letters also squash around their centre, not their baseline, so they never read as landing on a trampoline. / Vary each element's drop height, timing and spring values, and anchor squash to the bottom edge. Have the letters drop from above onto a clear baseline below the candy, with a real compress-and-launch bounce, and make the shapes visibly bump into their neighbours. Replace the 0.3s dead hold and the stiff whole-group rotation (4.2s to 5s) with staggered per-element idle wiggles: small offset rotation and scale loops on each letter and candy.
- **10-corporate-smooth**: The build sequence is muddled and generic. The panel starts fading in at 0.1s, before the accent line starts at 0.25s, so the line never leads. The 'soft mask/slide' reveal is only an opacity fade with a 12–16px nudge. The result reads as four layers fading in together, not a choreographed premium lower third. / Re-sequence it as line → panel → text. Grow the 3px line first (0–0.6s), then wipe or slide the panel out from behind it using a scale-x or mask reveal anchored at the line (0.4–1.2s). Then reveal the name and title through a real track-matte mask clipped to the panel, staggered about 0.15s apart. Mirror this order on the way out, so the line retracts last.
- **12-hand-drawn**: The hand-drawn boil is missing: wiggle(8, 0.5) on rotation is a smooth, near-invisible half-degree drift. Combined with the uniform 6px vector strokes and ±2% blob breathing, the piece reads as clean and mechanical instead of hand-animated at 12fps. / Add real stepped boil: wrap the path, scale and position wiggles in posterizeTime(12), cycle 2-3 slightly varied path keyframes per line (or animate stroke width at 12fps), and push the blob breathing to about 4-6% with mild non-uniform x/y scale so the shapes feel alive and organic.
- **15-minimal-luxury**: Georgia never loaded, so the hero wordmark renders in a generic fallback sans; the brief's wide-tracked serif, the main signal of luxury, is missing and ÉCLAT reads as a tech logo rather than a perfume house. / Embed or bundle a real refined serif (e.g. a Didone or high-contrast display serif at a light weight) and verify it renders. Then enlarge the 28px subline and add one quiet finishing touch (a slow sheen across the gold line or a gentle tracking settle on the subline) so the 2.2s end hold feels intentional instead of static.
- **17-music-logo-sting**: Apart from the 4.0s drop, the motion ignores the 120 BPM grid: the ring settles at 0.9s, the build pulses run in 0.25s steps that drift off the beat, the shockwave ends at 4.9s, the wordmark chars land around 5.1-5.3s, and the two secondary hits peak at 5.6s/6.1s, 3 frames after the 5.5s/6.0s beats. The comp is also 9.5s against an 8s brief, and the last 3.2s is a dead hold. / Snap every landing to the 0.5s grid: build pulses on 0.5/1.0/1.5..., ring settle and wordmark reveal finishing on 4.5s, secondary hit peaks exactly on 5.5s and 6.0s. Make the 0-4s build escalate (growing scale, faster subdivisions, a tension squash right before the drop), then trim the comp to 8s with roughly a 1.5s clean lockup hold.
