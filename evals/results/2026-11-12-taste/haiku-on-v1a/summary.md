## Judge critique

- **01-logo-reveal**: The tagline is undersized (40px under a 150px wordmark), dim #8fa3bf, and only fades to an arbitrary 80% opacity, so the third beat barely reads; the piece overall is a thin four-layer build with no secondary motion once the wordmark lands. / Enlarge the tagline (~56-64px with letter-spacing), bring it to full opacity, align its left edge exactly with the wordmark, and add one finishing touch such as a small slash-accent pulse or a subtle lockup scale settle to tie the beats together.
- **02-kinetic-quote**: Every phrase uses the same word fade-in, hold, then drift-up-and-fade exit, so the beats don't flow into each other: there are empty gaps (2.8–3.0s), long static holds (~1.5s on q1), and the comp runs 10s instead of 8s, so the attribution lands at 8.6s, past the brief's duration. / Retime to 8s and make each exit hand off to the next entrance (overlap q2's entry with q1's exit, and have 'Design is' push directly into 'how it works.'), then give 'how it works.' its own motion: per-letter scale/overshoot or a slam-in, with the underline driven by it, so the emphasis comes from movement as well as size and color.
- **03-toggle-micro**: The knob is a flat white ellipse with no drop shadow, the track has no depth, and the check simply trims on, so it reads as a correct but bare diagram of an iOS toggle rather than a polished micro-interaction. / Give the knob a soft offset shadow with a subtle press-in scale at 0.35s, and pop the checkmark in (scale 0→110→100 alongside the trim) while the green deepens slightly on settle, so each beat has secondary motion layered over the slide.
- **04-explainer-3beat**: The three-beat structure is badly unbalanced: the suck-in beat lasts under a second (3.6–4.54s), the payoff is crammed into 4.8–7.9s, and the last 4.07s is a dead static hold. The 'anxious jitter' is also only a gentle 3.5° rotation sine on a small pile, so it reads as calm sway rather than anxiety. / Retime to three real ~4s beats. Give beat 1 position and rotation jitter with higher amplitude and irregular timing. Stretch the suck-in to about 3s with staggered, curved paths that pull toward the shield while it scales up. Let the glow pulse, the check draw and the headline land across 8–11s, then add a short settle and secondary motion (glow breathing, subtle headline tracking) so the final second isn't a frozen frame.
- **05-bar-chart**: The build is correct but thin. Value labels only fade and nudge up instead of counting to their values, and the Q4 'pop' is a barely perceptible 4% Y-stretch, so the climax never lands. After 2.55s the only motion is a slow 3% whole-chart scale that drifts the baseline and labels without adding meaning. / Make the payoff the moment: count each label up in sync with its bar's growth, give Q4 a clear overshoot (around 110% with a spring settle) plus a brief accent such as a glow, a label scale-punch or a delta callout like '+80% vs Q3', and replace the drifting group scale with a deliberate hold.
- **06-loop-bg**: All four blobs share the same 0s→3s→6s A-B-A keyframes, so they ping-pong in lockstep along short straight lines; the field 'breathes' as one unit instead of drifting organically, and the blobs have no scale, opacity or hue variation. The dot grid is also off-center: it spans x 356–1684, giving 356px margin on the left vs 236px on the right, and leaves the lower-left a dead dark corner. / Give each blob a looping circular or elliptical path with sin/cos at 6s-divisible periods (e.g. 6s and 3s) and staggered phase offsets, plus a gentle scale/opacity breathe on its own phase. Then re-center the dot grid on 960,540 and drive the pulse phase from column and row (not layer index) so it reads as a clean diagonal wave.
- **07-product-promo**: After a decent 1s-per-word opening, the piece goes dead: PULSE 2 slams at 3s then sits frozen for ~2.6s until 6s, and after the pill pops in it holds another ~2s. The feature words also exit with near-invisible 0.05s nudges plus hard opacity cuts, so the 'energetic transitions' and 120 BPM drive never really happen, and the 'Available now' pill is undersized and weak in hierarchy. / Re-cut the back half to the 0.5s grid: add beat-synced accents in the 3–6s hold (a second ring, a scale pulse or shake on the slam, glow or tracking on PULSE 2) and bring 'Available now' in by ~4.5s as a larger pill. Give each feature word a real 0.15–0.2s exit (slide or scale with motion blur, a flash wipe) that overlaps the next word's entrance, instead of cutting on opacity.
- **09-playful-bouncy**: The letters drop from y=260 straight through the candy row, so B covers the coral circle and the "!" punches through the lilac star (0.67s of text-on-text and shape collisions). The final lockup also sits top-heavy, leaving the bottom ~40% of the frame empty, and the piece runs 6s instead of the briefed 5s. / Move the whole composition down about 150px. Launch the letters from below or from behind the candy row so they never cross the shapes, and replace the hand-keyed Quad bounces with a real spring or Back overshoot plus one candy-to-candy bump reaction, so the elasticity and jostle actually read. Trim the comp to 5s.
- **10-corporate-smooth**: The 'soft mask' reveal is never built: the Text mask group has no matte, so name and title just fade and slide 34px through open space. The outro then dumps the text with short 0.22-0.45s Cubic In fades starting at ~3.95s, which feels rushed against the calm 0.7-0.8s intro. The result is a generic fade-slide lower third, and the 780px panel is half empty on the right. / Add a real track-matte mask (panel bounds, or a rect wiping with the accent line) so the text rises from behind an edge with no opacity crutch. Lengthen the exits to ~0.6s Cubic In Out, staggered title→name→panel→line, and push the hold to ~4.3s. Tighten the panel width to the text (~560px) so the card feels designed rather than default.
- **12-hand-drawn**: The 'hand-drawn' feel is mostly absent: the boil is a smooth wiggle(12, 0.5) on rotation (half a degree, not stepped at 12fps, no path deformation), the blobs breathe at ±1.2% (invisible), and the wordmark is a stock bold serif with a plain per-character fade, so from about 2.9s to 6s the frame reads as a static, slightly sterile layout with tiny wheat and loaf drawings floating among corner-scattered blobs. / Make the imperfection visible and on the beat: posterize the boil to 12fps (posterizeTime(12)) and drive it through 2–3 alternating path keyframes or a displacement wiggle on the strokes and the blob outlines, raise the blob breathing to roughly 4–6% with a slight rotation drift, and swap the type for a rounded or handwritten face that bounces in on a soft overshoot, so the back half of the 6s has life instead of a 3-second static hold.
- **15-minimal-luxury**: The gold rule sits at y=900, inside the letters' cap-height zone, so it reads as a stray strikethrough clipping the tops of L and A rather than a deliberate accent. The lockup also feels cramped: the line, name and subtitle are stacked tight in the vertical center, with no structure using the 1920px height. / Move the rule clear of the type, e.g. ~60-80px below the name between it and EAU DE PARFUM, or well above it as a horizon. Keep its slow center-out draw but let it finish after the last letter lands. Give the subtitle more air, and add a subtle sustained secondary motion through the 2.3s end hold, like a slow overall tracking settle or a soft champagne sheen across the line, so the expensive feel carries to the end.
- **17-music-logo-sting**: The drop and the beats after it are underpowered. The hero hit is mostly a 150% dot spring and a 112% ring pop. The wordmark arrives late as a soft per-character fade starting at 4.1 s, and the three ripples (3px #ff3b5c at 60% on near-black) barely show. Meanwhile every half-beat in the build and every beat after the drop gets the same small dot bump, so nothing stands out and the restraint check fails. / Drop the dot pulse on alternate beats and keep the ring idle from 1.4 to 3 s so the accelerating 3–4 s build carries the anticipation. Land a much bigger moment exactly at 4.0 s: a thick, bright ripple, a short flash or scale punch on the whole lockup, and the wordmark sliding or masking out from behind the mark on the drop. Then keep only two clearly visible secondary hits, at 4.5 and 5.0 s, before a still lockup hold.
