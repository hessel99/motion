## Judge critique

- **01-logo-reveal**: The sequence is correct but thin: the ring draws, the slash snaps on, the wordmark slides, and the tagline fades, with no secondary motion, no response from the mark when the wordmark leaves it, and a hand-keyed overshoot (351→306→320) that reads as a wobble rather than a settle. The final lockup also sits cramped, with NOVA almost touching the ring and a tiny, low-contrast tagline. / Add one connecting beat: when the slash lands, give the mark a small scale or rotation pop, and launch the wordmark from that impact with a single spring settle instead of the manual bounce. Then open the gap between mark and wordmark by about 30px, and make the tagline larger and brighter so the end hold reads as a balanced lockup.
- **02-kinetic-quote**: The payoff "how it works." and its underline exit at 7.15–7.45s, leaving a nearly empty frame with only a tiny gray attribution as the final image. There is also a dead blank beat around 5.1s, and every phrase hands off with the same static hold and Expo-In drift-and-fade, so the piece reads as a slideshow, not kinetic type. / Hold the red payoff and underline through to 8s with the attribution settling beneath it. Close the 5.0–5.2s gap by having "Design is" carry directly into "how it works.", for example by keeping "Design is" on screen and pushing it up while the payoff words spring in below. Give each transition its own motion, such as a per-letter exit or a word-by-word wipe.
- **03-toggle-micro**: The knob motion is mistimed: Back Out puts the knob almost at its end position by 0.55s, so the 118/88 stretch peaks when the knob arrives instead of mid-travel. The impact squash (97/103) is too faint to register. Meanwhile the grey→green fill crossfades in RGB through a muddy grey-green at 0.55s, so the switch's key moment looks dirty instead of snappy. / Re-time the deformation to the travel: stretch peaks around 0.5s mid-slide, then a clear horizontal compression (about 90/110) lands at contact around 0.65s before the overshoot settles. Add a short press anticipation on the knob at 0.35–0.4s. Speed up the track fill (0.4–0.6s) with an intermediate key so it passes through a clean saturated tone instead of desaturated grey-green.
- **04-explainer-3beat**: Beat 1 has no anxiety: the notes sit dead still at fixed positions for ~3s, with only a 1.5px/2.5° wiggle that doesn't read on the frames. The shield scales in at 0.4s on top of the pile, hiding 'hunter2'/'qwerty' and giving away the reveal. Beat 2 is then crammed into ~1s (3.6–4.7s), leaving 4.7–7s as glow breathing on an empty shield. / Keep the shield hidden until ~3.4s and give the notes real nervous motion: 6–10px jittered position, ±6–8° rotation, small scale twitches, and a slow drift that tightens the pile. Stretch the suck-in to ~3.5–6s with a slower lead-in, a squash/flash on the shield at each impact, and a stronger glow burst. Then land the check with a scale pop at ~6.5s and the headline right after, so beat 3 doesn't idle.
- **05-bar-chart**: All motion is over by 2.7s and the remaining 3.3s is a near-dead hold (a 2% camera drift that reads as nothing), while the build itself is uniform Expo Out fades/scales: value labels only fade instead of counting up, the bars' bottom-anchoring isn't guaranteed (checker flags it), and the Q4 'pop' is a barely visible 4% Y-scale bump. / Re-pace the build across ~4.5s with the bar anchors locked to the baseline, count the values up in sync with each bar's growth, and give Q4 a real accent beat (overshoot spring on height plus a scale/colour pulse on the $27M label, with the other bars dimming slightly) so the highlight lands and the end hold is earned.
- **06-loop-bg**: The blobs bunch into two overlapping clumps that drift off the top and bottom-left edges, leaving large dead dark areas. Where purple and teal overlap they mix into a muddy grey-blue. The 2π/3-rate orbits on violet and cyan move at about 200px/s, which reads restless rather than 'slow drift'. The dot grid is 8px at 5–55% opacity and almost invisible on a stage screen. / Spread the five orbs across the full frame with their centers kept on-canvas. Drop every orbit to the base 2π/6 frequency with smaller amplitudes (≤80px), and add a screen/add blend so overlaps glow instead of muddying. Bump the dots to 10–12px with a 20–70% opacity range so the diagonal pulse wave actually reads.
- **07-product-promo**: After the slam lands at ~3.85s, almost nothing moves for the remaining 6s: the frame sits static from 4.5s to 7s, and again from 7.5s to the end. The feature words also exit by hold-cutting to zero opacity, not with real transitions. Together these drain the 'fast, energetic, 120 BPM' feel. The only connective motion is a full-screen flash and a single 125→100 scale punch repeated three times. / Give each feature word a directional exit tied to the beat, such as a whip or slide with motion blur, a mask wipe, or a per-character stagger off on the next downbeat. After the slam, keep the beat alive with layered secondary motion: a second delayed ring, a subtle scale throb on PULSE 2 every 0.5s, and the rule extending to the full title width. Bring 'Available now' in around 5–5.5s with a stagger on the pill and its text, so the end hold is about 1.5s rather than 2.5s of dead frame.
- **09-playful-bouncy**: Every candy and every letter runs the same cloned keyframe recipe: one drop, one small rebound, a uniform 25px sidestep, then a single-arc letter hop. The motion reads as mechanical and rigid rather than elastic. The 'jostle' never shows shapes pushing each other, the 'trampoline' is one up-and-down with no rebound, and the ±4° idle rotation is too faint to read as a wiggle. / Break the cloning and give the letters real trampoline physics. Have each letter sink into a squash, launch, land, and rebound at least once more with decaying height (spring with lower damping). Vary drop heights, bounce counts and jostle directions per shape so neighbours visibly knock into each other. Add scale breathing and a larger offset wiggle to the idle so the end loop feels alive.
- **10-corporate-smooth**: The build order and reveal don't match the brief: the panel fades in at 0.1s before the accent line starts at 0.6s, and the 'mask' groups don't appear to clip. At 1.1s the half-faded name slides up through the accent line, so the reveal is just a generic fade-plus-slide that briefly collides with the line instead of a clean soft-mask wipe. / Lead with the accent line (left-anchored scaleX, ~0.8s Quart In-Out), then slide the panel out from it and reveal name and title through real clipping masks above and below the line, with no opacity crossfade. Mirror that sequence on the way out with matching in-out eases instead of the abrupt Cubic In, and add a subtle panel edge or gradient so the 72% translucency actually reads.
- **12-hand-drawn**: The 'hand-drawn boil' is effectively invisible: wiggle(10, 0.9) is a sub-pixel, smooth 24fps position jitter rather than a stepped 12fps line boil, and the paths are near-straight vectors (the stalk is a 5px-wide plumb line). Combined with a layout that pushes the small wheat and loaf to the far left and right edges, leaves a dead empty centre, and jams the wordmark and underline against the bottom safe area (underline at y≈1008-1020), it reads as a sparse, mechanical vector sketch, not an organic illustration. / Recompose into a centred lockup with the wheat and loaf enlarged and grouped above the wordmark, which should sit within title-safe. Then make the boil real: add posterizeTime(12) to the strokes and apply a 2-3px wiggle to path vertices (or 3-4 cycled hand-drawn path keys), with uneven, tapered stroke widths, so the lines look hand-animated.
- **15-minimal-luxury**: The subline ends with a fast micro-bounce (1052→1044.4→1045.25→1045 in 0.24s steps), an overshoot that breaks the 'nothing moves fast' rule. It sits as a small, untracked 34px caption crammed under the name, so the second beat reads like an afterthought. Beyond that, the piece is only a line draw plus fades, with no luxury detail. / Replace the subline settle with a single ≥1.2s Sine Out drift and no overshoot. Widen its tracking and give it more space below the name. Then add one restrained finishing touch, such as a slow gold sheen sweep across ÉCLAT or the line gently extending after the name lands, to fill the 1.6s opening and the end hold.
- **17-music-logo-sting**: The 4-second build is visually starved: a lone white dot and a hairline 3px arc on an empty black frame give the anticipation almost no weight, so the drop reads as the start of the piece rather than its payoff, and the secondary hits at 5.5s and 6.0s are only 4% scale bumps with faint 2px rings. / Make the build escalate: thicken and brighten the trim ring, add a pre-drop pull-back or squash on the dot around 3.6–3.9s, then hit the drop with a bolder shockwave (thicker, brighter, maybe a second ring and a brief background flash). Push the 5.5s and 6.0s hits to roughly 8–10% scale with more visible rings, and centre the full lockup on the frame, since it currently sits about 70px right of centre.
