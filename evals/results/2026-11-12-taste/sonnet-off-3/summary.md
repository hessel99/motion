## Judge critique

- **01-logo-reveal**: The reveal is competent but generic: the mark simply traces on and stops, the wordmark does one Expo Out slide-plus-fade, and the tagline drifts up, so nothing has secondary motion, settle or connection between beats. The 38px tagline is also undersized against the 150px wordmark and the lockup sits right of centre, so the final hold reads thin and slightly unbalanced. / Give the assembly a payoff: when the slash completes at ~1.35s, add a small scale pop or rotation settle on the mark (overshoot spring), and let that impulse push the wordmark out with a slight overshoot. Then centre the full lockup, enlarge or letter-space the tagline, and remove the unused #6b8cff slash fill so the palette stays navy, white and one green.
- **02-kinetic-quote**: The p1→p2 handoff collides: p2's words start entering at 1.9s while 'Design is not' is still at full opacity until 2.0s and only clears by 2.2s, so at ~2.17s the two phrases sit on top of each other ('Dejsignwisatnot'). This, plus 1.5s+ static holds on each early phrase, makes the transitions read as an overlap glitch followed by dead air, not as phrases flowing into each other. / Choreograph a true handoff. Have p1 exit fully (by word, upward, ~0.4s) before p2 starts, or let p2 enter from below in the space p1 vacates. Trim the static holds to ~0.8s and spend the saved time on a held, scaled-up landing for 'how it works.' Also fix the tight 'howit' word spacing in the final lockup.
- **03-toggle-micro**: The motion is a minimal four-key slide: there is no press or anticipation beat, the overshoot is a single hand-keyed bounce (461→452) instead of a spring, and the 120/90 squash at mid-travel turns the knob into a flat egg while the track sits at a washed-out half-green, so the 0.55s frame looks glitchy rather than juicy. / Replace the position and scale keys with a spring (or a 2-oscillation decaying overshoot), cap the squash at about 108/94 tied to velocity, and add a short 0.35–0.4s press-down plus a knob drop shadow that shifts with the slide, so the interaction feels physical and tactile.
- **04-explainer-3beat**: The 'suck-in' is a slow, evenly spaced queue: each note eases to center one at a time on 0.3s offsets with Easy Ease position, so beat 2 reads as polite filing rather than a vacuum pull. The 'glow' is a 14%-opacity lavender disc that looks like a flat grey plate on the cream background, so beat 3 lands without a payoff. / Rebuild the absorption as an accelerating pull: compress the stagger toward the end (e.g. 0.3s → 0.08s), use an expo-in position curve with a slight curved/orbiting path and rotation spin-up, and let the shield squash per hit. Then make the glow a bright, warm, soft-edged radial burst (higher opacity, blur/gradient, quick bloom then settle) so the check and headline feel earned.
- **05-bar-chart**: All motion ends at 3.2s, leaving a 2.8s static hold (almost half the piece), and every move uses the same Expo Out fade/scale, so the chart has a clean build but no payoff beat or sense of rhythm after Q4 lands. / Slow the build so it fills about 0–4.5s, with Q4 landing near 4s. Make the values count up from 0 in sync with each bar's growth. Then give Q4 a real accent beat: a slight overshoot squash on the bar, a label pop, and a soft glow or dim of Q1–Q3. End on a short ~1s hold.
- **06-loop-bg**: The piece is technically correct but thin: five orbs on near-identical one-cycle sine paths plus two checkerboard dot groups crossfading in lockstep. The motion reads as a mechanical wobble, the frame is unbalanced (the orb mass sits low and to the sides, leaving a dead dark band across the top-center), and where o3 overlaps the teal orbs the colors turn a muddy blue. / Add depth while keeping the 6s loop. Give each orb a distinct loop-safe path (mix 1x/2x/3x harmonics with varied phases), a slow scale or opacity breathe, and a gentle hue drift between purple and teal. Stagger the dot pulse with a per-dot phase offset (for example a radial or diagonal wave) instead of two global crossfading groups. Rebalance orb placement so the top-center isn't empty.
- **07-product-promo**: The second half loses the 120 BPM energy. After the slam settles at 4.6s nothing moves until the pill at 6.5s, the pill's spring segment (100→100) does nothing, and the last ~3s is a static hold. Before that, 3.5–4s is an empty dead beat with only a shrinking dot, so the drop arrives late instead of on the beat. / Re-cut the back half to the 0.5s grid: slam PULSE 2 on the beat right after w3 exits (3.5s), land the pill at 5s with a real overshoot, then keep small beat-synced accents through the hold, such as a pill pulse every 0.5s, a subtle name scale or glow throb, and a second ring echo.
- **09-playful-bouncy**: The 'Boop!' wordmark is crammed into the middle row with no breathing room: the green circle butts against the B, the ! nearly touches the purple square, and the letters collide mid-hop (o2 over o1 around 2.7s). The hero word reads as one more element in a crowded grid, not the payoff. / Give the logo its own space: scale it up, center it with clear margins, and push the candy shapes outward or behind it. Swap the hand-keyed Quad bounces for real springs or Back overshoot, with staggered contact-driven jostles rather than one identical sideways nudge per shape, so it actually feels elastic.
- **10-corporate-smooth**: The piece is a bare-minimum template: one line, one flat panel, and two text layers with Cubic In Out fades and slides. The panel drifts 80px on its own track, so mid-intro its left edge is misaligned with the static accent line (see 1.10s), and the exit compresses line retract, panel fade and both text exits into the same 0.8s. / Make the line drive the build. Have the panel wipe down or out from the line's left anchor in sync with it rather than sliding independently, and use longer (0.8–1s) eases on the opacity ramps. Then give the out a clear stagger: text first, then panel, then the line retracting last. Optionally add one premium detail, such as a soft gradient or inner highlight on the panel.
- **12-hand-drawn**: The hand-drawn feel is only skin-deep: the lines are clean, uniform-weight vectors and the only 'boil' is a 3px whole-group jitter at 12fps, so nothing actually wobbles per-line. The Fredoka fallback renders as a stiff geometric sans, and leftover default #6b8cff blue fills flash on the stem (~1.08s) and underline (~4.38s). / Add real per-path boil by cycling 2-3 slightly offset path keyframes (or a stepped per-vertex wiggle) on every stroke at 12fps with varied stroke width. Also set the stem, scores and underline fills to none, and make sure the rounded friendly font actually loads so the name lands warm instead of corporate.
- **15-minimal-luxury**: Cormorant Garamond failed to load, so ÉCLAT renders in a generic sans fallback. That removes the serif luxury voice the brief depends on, and tracking never actually tightens: only a 104→100 scale stands in for it. / Embed/preload Cormorant Garamond and add a real tracking animator per character (e.g. +40 → +20 over the same 1.6s Sine Out stagger), then let the line's lower end subtly settle or glint as the name lands so the three beats feel connected rather than three isolated fades.
- **17-music-logo-sting**: Nothing lands on the grid: the hero settles at 4.4–4.45s (ring Expo Out, dot Back Out) instead of on the 4.0s drop, and every build pulse and secondary hit peaks 0.08–0.12s after its beat (0.62, 1.12, 5.6, 6.12…). The sync reads as late throughout. The broken ring trim-on also leaves the build as just a fade, and the wordmark drifts in with a soft 0.8s Easy Ease well after the drop. / Re-time the peaks to the 0.5s grid: put the ring/dot impact and the wordmark's arrival frame exactly at 4.0s, with the anticipation squash ending at 3.97s, and land each build pulse and secondary hit on its beat with a fast attack and a Quad Out decay after it. Then replace the non-rendering trim with a working draw-on so the build accelerates into the drop.
