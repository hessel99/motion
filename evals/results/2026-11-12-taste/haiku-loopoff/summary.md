## Judge critique

- **01-logo-reveal**: The wordmark does not slide out from behind the mark as the brief asks: it is drawn above the mark with no mask and simply fades in while nudging 80px right, so at 1.45s the half-opaque 'NOVA' sits on top of the ring instead of emerging from it. / Put the wordmark under a clip or mask whose left edge sits at the ring's right edge, start the text fully hidden behind the mark at opacity 100, and slide it out about 250px with the Quart Out ease, which delivers the key reveal beat and gives the piece the craft moment it lacks.
- **02-kinetic-quote**: The phrases never transition into each other: each one blinks out with a 0.15s Expo In opacity cut, leaving blank frames (2.3–2.5s, 4.3–4.5s, 5.5–5.7s, visible empty at 4.37s), while 'Design is' holds only ~0.4s and 'Design is not just' sits static for over a second, so the rhythm is stop-start rather than kinetic. / Overlap the beats: drive each exit as a per-word stagger (up/blur out) that starts as the next phrase's words begin entering, so the incoming words push the outgoing ones off. Rebalance the holds by trimming the p1/p2 dwell and giving 'Design is' a beat that lands into 'how it works.' to remove the dead gaps and build momentum toward the red payoff.
- **03-toggle-micro**: The interaction reads flat and thin: the knob has no drop shadow or depth, so it looks unlike an iOS knob, and the overshoot is only 4px (453→449), so it barely registers. The checkmark is a separate, unparented layer, so it ignores the knob's landing squash at 0.84s and draws on with no scale or pop. / Parent the check to the knob so it inherits the squash, and add a soft knob shadow. Push the overshoot to about 10–12px with a spring settle, and give the check a quick scale pop (0.6→1.1→1) alongside the trim reveal. Add a brief press anticipation (knob widening) just before 0.4s.
- **04-explainer-3beat**: The whole illustration is tiny: notes of 57–108px and a 220px shield sit in a ~250px cluster in the middle of a 1920x1080 frame. Beat 1 reads as a small static clump, because the 'anxious jitter' is only a ±5° rotation sine with no positional shake, and the notes pile on top of each other so their dots and labels collide. After the headline lands at 8.6s, the last ~3.4s is a near-dead hold with only a 3% glow pulse. / Scale the scene up about 2.5–3x and spread the notes across the frame with real wiggle on position and rotation. Then make the suck-in a staggered curved-path pull with squash, ending in a shield impact pulse and ring burst. Fill beat 3 with a proper glow bloom, a check that pops with overshoot, and a staggered word-by-word headline that resolves by about 10s.
- **05-bar-chart**: All motion finishes by ~2.5s, leaving a 3.5s near-static hold. The only life after that is a barely perceptible 2% Q4 scale drift, which also creeps the bar top up toward the '27' label. Value labels only fade and rise instead of counting up, so the data reveal feels flat and the piece reads as generic. / Spread the build across the 6s with slower bar grows and a wider stagger. Have the value labels count up from 0 in sync with each bar's growth, then give Q4 a clear accent beat around 3-3.5s (a color sweep from grey to blue, or a label pop with a subtle glow or delta callout) before a deliberate final hold. Drop the scale drift or pin the label to the bar top.
- **06-loop-bg**: All three blobs orbit on the same 6s sine period with similar amplitudes, so they read as one rigid cluster rotating mechanically in the upper-left/centre; the right third and bottom of the frame often sit as empty navy, and the dot grid is so faint it barely registers. / Spread the orbs across the full 16:9 frame and give each its own loop-safe frequency (1x, 2x, 3x of 6s) with mixed x/y periods and larger scale and opacity breathing, then raise the dot pulse floor and peak so the grid's wave is actually visible without getting busy.
- **07-product-promo**: The energy stops after the 3s slam. The shockwave ring is a thin, dim stroke that is gone by 3.7s, then PULSE 2 sits nearly still from 3.4s to 6s and again from 6.5s to 10s. Also, the ring sits at scale 10 and opacity 100 before its 3s key, so a small orange circle shows from frame 0 (visible at f0 and behind 'Spatial audio'). / Hide the ring until 3s with an opacity hold, and fill the second half on the 0.5s grid: a beat-synced pulse or shake on PULSE 2, a second echo ring, and a staggered pop for the pill and text. Also give the feature words real exits (a punch-out or whip that matches each entrance) instead of a one-frame opacity cut.
- **09-playful-bouncy**: Every candy shape and every letter runs the same copy-pasted keyframe recipe (identical drop, two bounces, squash values, and a ±14px 'jostle' that is just a timed wiggle in place), so nothing actually collides or reacts to anything else. The motion reads mechanical rather than elastic, and the comp runs 6s against a 5s brief. / Make the jostle physical and the timing tighter. Vary each shape's drop height, bounce count and squash amount by size. When a shape lands, have it nudge its neighbour so they push sideways in opposite directions. Let the letters' landing kick the shape cluster above them. Compress everything to 5s so the 'Boop!' hop and its idle wiggle (bigger than ±3-4°, with a scale breathe) fill the last 1.2s.
- **10-corporate-smooth**: The reveals are generic fade-plus-nudge moves rather than the soft mask/slide the brief asked for: the panel only drifts 40px while fading, so for its first and last frames its left edge sits about 36px left of the accent line, and the name and title just fade up 24px with no mask, so the 'line extends, panel emerges from behind it' idea never comes through. / Make the accent line the origin of everything: keep the panel's left edge anchored at the line and reveal it with a left-to-right mask or scale-X wipe from the line over ~0.8s, then reveal the name and title through a mask clipped to the panel interior (slide up 30–40px under the mask, title offset ~0.2s) and reverse that order on exit, finishing by ~5.6s so there is a short clean empty hold at the end.
- **12-hand-drawn**: It doesn't feel hand-drawn: the 'boil' is a 0.9px position jitter on rigid, clean vector strokes that dies out by 4.9s, the wheat and loaf are tidy geometric icons, and the name is set in Inter Bold, so the piece reads as a clean corporate logo rather than an organic, imperfect bakery mark. / Add real line boil: 3-4 slightly varied path keyframes per stroke, held and cycling at 12fps, plus uneven stroke width. Keep it running through the end hold, and swap Inter for a rounded, hand-lettered face whose letters settle in with a small per-character rotation and bounce.
- **15-minimal-luxury**: The piece is technically on-brief but thin. Six layers fade in with no light, sheen or depth, so it reads as a template rather than expensive. The 0.6s subline fade breaks the 'nothing fast' rule. The barely-perceptible 14px tracking shift and the short line hugging the cap height make the lockup feel cramped rather than airy. / Raise the craft: run the line draw longer (~3.5s) and place it with real breathing room above the name. Make the tracking tighten clearly visible (e.g. 40-60px over 4s with a soft blur-to-sharp per letter). Stretch the subline fade to ≥1.2s, and finish with a slow champagne light sweep across the letters during the end hold.
- **17-music-logo-sting**: The drop has little impact: the build is almost static (the ring only creeps from 72 to 78% scale over 2.4s while the dot does small beat pulses), and on the 4.0s drop only the mark gives an 8% bump while the wordmark starts 0.2s late with a scrappy per-character reveal (stray glyph fragments at 4.37s), so the drop has no clear hero moment. / Make the build escalate into the drop: tighten or contract the mark and accelerate or enlarge the dot pulses through 3.0–3.9s. Then land everything together at exactly 4.0s: the wordmark wipes or slides out from behind the mark starting on the drop, with a strong first shockwave ring and a brief flash, and the 4.5/5.0/5.5 echo rings stay as the smaller secondary hits.
