# Y2K chrome
> Glossy millennium optimism: liquid chrome, iridescent pastels, starbursts, sparkles and smooth spinning stickers.

**Use for**: fashion drops, music, beauty, Gen-Z campaigns, retro-future promos.

## Palette
- Background `#0B0B2B` (deep indigo) or `#E9E4FF` (lilac).
- Chrome greys `#F5F5F5`, `#BFC5D2`, `#7D8597`; iridescent accents `#FF8AD8` pink, `#8AF3FF` aqua, `#C6FF8A` lime, `#B58CFF` purple.

## Shapes
Starbursts and 4-point sparkles (paths), pills, rounded stars, outlined bubble shapes; "chrome" by stacking: base grey + white highlight copy offset −4 px + darker edge copy offset +4 px. Orbits (thin ellipses).

## Typography
Inter 900, 120–200 px, letterSpacing −0.03 × size, slanted with rotation −6°; or outlines (stroke 4 px, fill transparent on paths). `monospace` 24 px for "tech" labels.

## Easing & timing
Family: `Back Out`, `spring Quick`, `Expo Out`, `Linear` for spins. Moves 0.4–0.7 s, staggers 0.05–0.08 s, sparkle twinkles 0.3–0.5 s.

## Signature moves
- Sparkle twinkle: scale 0 → 120 → 0 with rotation 0 → 90 (`Back Out`, then `Back In`), random delays.
- Chrome sweep: a white highlight bar slides across the logo, opacity 0 → 80 → 0.
- Spinning sticker: rotation expression `time*90` + a gentle scale pulse.
- Orbit: small spheres in a parent group that rotates (`Linear`).

## Transitions
Starburst zoom (a sparkle scales to fill the frame with `Expo In`), or a quick iridescent wipe.

## Do / Don't
- Do: shine, layering, sparkles at focal points, playful spins.
- Don't: flat corporate type, desaturated colors, slow heavy timing.

## Mini scene plan (8 s)
1. 0–2.5 s: a chrome star spins in from scale 0 with `Back Out`; sparkles twinkle around it.
2. 2.5–5.5 s: "NEW DROP" pops by letter; a chrome sweep passes over it.
3. 5.5–8 s: spheres orbit the logo; final sparkle burst; hold.
