# Logo recipes
> Logo stings from a study of 249 professional logo animations: timing by pace, the easing families, the wordmark entry, and 25 recipes with exact tool calls (dots converge, write-on, morph to letters, pull-back, hinge, mirror exit…).

How to use: `inspect_artwork` the mark first and name what it depicts (get_guide "concepts"), pick ONE concept family that comes from the mark's own form or meaning (concepts §2), then take the matching recipe below. Times are for a 1920×1080 sting; `<mark>`, `<word>`… are layer ids. Rigs are editable keys (`rig {kind, ids, t0, duration, intensity, mode, options}`). Every recipe ends on the exact lockup held 1.5–2 s; add a mirrored exit (recipe 25) when the sting must leave or loop.

## Timing (medians of 123 stings)
| pace / mood | total | lockup complete | hold | wordmark after the mark lands |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| all | 6.0 s (IQR 4.8–8.0) | 2.7 s | 1.8 s | 0.7 s (IQR 0.5–1.1) |
| fast / energetic | 5.1 s | 2.6 s | 1.65 s | 0.45 s |
| medium | 6.4 s | 3.0 s | 1.95 s | 0.7 s |
| calm | 6.2 s | 2.5 s | 1.8 s | 0.6 s |
| elegant / professional | 7.4–7.5 s | 3.3 s | 2.0–2.35 s | 0.8 s |
| the best-rated pieces | 5.0 s | 2.6 s | 1.25 s | – |

Defaults: a 0.2–0.3 s blank (or still) lead-in, almost nothing starts on frame 0 (median first motion 0.27 s); the hero idea done by ~2.5 s; the wordmark 0.5–0.7 s after the mark lands (0.45 fast, 0.8 elegant); hold 1.5–2 s (elegant 2.5); total ~5–6 s (~4 s for social). Only ~30 % of the time is in motion. The build is 1.5–3 s whatever the mood: mood changes the eases and the hold, not the build length. With sound, a hit lands within 0.3 s of the lock.

## Easing families (pick ONE per piece)
1. **Snappy expo** (tech, confident, most stings): `Expo Out` / `Quart Out` arrivals of 0.2–0.4 s, then dead still; move-hold-move with 0.15–0.3 s holds.
2. **Soft sine** (elegant, calm, luxury): 0.6–1.2 s `Sine In Out`, letter staggers 0.3–0.4 s per word, no overshoot, long holds.
3. **Springs** (playful, friendly): visible overshoot, 1–2 wobbles and a squash on impact: `spring Bouncy`, `spring Quick`, `Back Out`, `secondary_motion` overshoot 0.08–0.15.
4. **Elastic** (playful type): letterforms stretch and snap back (recipe 15): `Sine In Out` out, `spring Wobbly` back.
5. **Stepped / hold** (pixel, tech, typewriter): `interp: "hold"` keys, `rig type-on`.
Rules: accelerate into a transformation and snap out of it; followers reuse the lead's ease 0.03–0.1 s later; exits mirror the entrance with `Expo In` and are faster (~0.7×); the final move is small (a 20–60 px nudge, a settle).

## Wordmark entry (what the 123 stings did)
After the mark settles, as its own beat: 48. The wordmark IS the mark: 33. Present throughout: 13. **Slides out from behind / beside the mark under a mask while the mark moves aside: 11 — the signature of the polished pieces.** Inside the mark: 9. None: 9. 50 of 123 animate back out (a mirror of the build). Default: recipe 2. Never fade the wordmark in on top of the finished mark.

## Recipes
1. **Dots converge and snap** (dot / petal / module marks; community, network): `rig {kind: "assemble", ids: [<dots>], t0: 0.25, duration: 0.6–0.9, intensity: 0.5, options: {order: "outside-in"}}`; equal distances start together, so re-order them with `choreograph {ids, pattern: "sequential", step: 0.07}` (hub last); optional swirl first: the dots' group `animate` rotation 90 → 0 with ease: "Expo Out" 0.6 s. Then the links `draw_on {ids: [<lines>], duration: 0.3–0.45, ease: "Expo Out", stagger: 0.05}`. Example: logo-dots-converge.
2. **The mark moves aside, the wordmark slides out from behind it** (the default wordmark entry): ~0.4–0.5 s after the mark lands, `rig {kind: "reveal-from", ids: [<word>], t0, duration: 0.6, options: {from: <mark>}}` (mask at the mark's edge, a small push back on the mark). To also move the mark to the lockup position: the mark's group `enter: {from: {dx: 200}, duration: 0.6, ease: "Cubic In Out"}` (a build_scene field), a mask rect (`mask: true`) whose left edge sits at the mark's right edge with the same enter, and the word inside that group with `enter: {from: {dx: -500}, duration: 0.7, ease: "Expo Out"}`. Letter variant: text `textAnim: {by: "char", stagger: 0.04, duration: 0.35, ease: "Expo Out", from: {opacity: 0, dx: -30}}`. Examples: logo-dots-converge, logo-giant-crop.
3. **Continuous monoline write-on** (script, monoline, rounded wordmarks; craft, signature): one path drawn at an even pen speed: `draw_on {ids: [<line>], t: 0.25, duration: 1.6–2.2, ease: "Sine In Out"}`; flourish at the end: `animate` rotation 0 → −3 → 0 with ease: "Back Out" as the pen lifts. A wordmark set in type is written with a **path-following mask**: a stroke wider than the letters (`strokeWidth` 1.3× the cap height, `mask: true`, under the text in the same group) along the word's direction, `drawOn: {duration: 0.9–1.1, ease: "Sine In Out"}`. Example: logo-monoline-write.
4. **A leading dot draws the ring, then the ring turns into the letter**: `draw_on {ids: [<ring>], duration: 0.8, ease: "Cubic In Out"}`; the dot rides the trim end: put the dot in a group whose pivot is the ring's centre (`set_pivot {id: <dotGroup>, at: [cx, cy]}`), dot placed at the ring's start point, and `animate` the group's rotation 0 → 360 with the same times and ease: "Cubic In Out"; then `animate` the ring's rotation 0 → 45 with ease: "Expo Out" over 0.5 s, then recipe 2.
5. **Comet trail flashes into a glyph**: an arc path; `draw_on {ids: [<arc>], duration: 0.9, ease: "Sine In Out"}` (turns trim paths on), then `set_keyframes {prop: "trimEnd"}` 0 → 100 and `set_keyframes {prop: "trimStart"}` 0 → 92 (the tail lags 8 %) over 0.9 s with ease: "Sine In Out"; the head: a small ellipse with `blur` 6, keyed along the arc (position keys with sOut/sIn tangents); at the end a flash ellipse `animate` scale 0 → 160 and opacity 100 → 0 in 0.25 s; the glyph pops with ease: "spring Quick".
6. **One dot splits into the mark** (symmetric marks, 2–4 identical parts): one part pops (scale 0 → 40, ease: "spring Bouncy", t 0.25), the others wait hidden under it at 40 % (opacity hold keys); at ~0.8 s each flies to its place: position from the centre + scale 40 → 100, ease: "Expo Out", 0.3–0.5 s, 0.08 s apart; then `secondary_motion {ids, props: ["position"], overshoot: 0.08, settle: 0.25}`. Example: logo-dot-splits.
7. **Shapes morph into letters** (modular / geometric wordmarks; architecture, construction): placeholder blocks at each letter's box enter (Expo Out, varied distances), hold as abstract shapes, then `morph {id: <block>, to: {d: <glyph path in the block's box>}, t: 1.0 + 0.07·i, duration: 0.45, ease: "Expo Out"}` (springs are not allowed on shape keys); a fill change carries it (`animate` fill, ease: "Cubic In Out"); final small move: letters close up 10–20 px. If the structures differ a lot, cross-fade two layers instead. Example: logo-blocks-morph.
8. **Word → monogram (and back)** (long names with an initials monogram): `split_text {id: <word>, by: "char"}`; the non-initials `animate` opacity → 0 and scale → 80 over 0.3 s, ordered with `choreograph {pattern: "edges-in", step: 0.02}`; the initials close the gap: `animate` position, 0.6 s, ease: "Cubic In Out"; after a 1.5 s hold, the same keys backwards.
9. **Letters as eyes** (round counters, dots; friendly brands): the o's / dots get pupils; pupils glance ±12–20 px (`animate` position, 0.25 s, ease: "Cubic In Out", hold 0.4 s), `rig {kind: "blink", ids: [<eyes>, <pupils>], options: {count: 2}}`, then pull back to the word (`rig {kind: "frame-detail", options: {at: [between the eyes], zoom: 4}}` with t0 after the blink); the pupils snap back to the centre with ease: "Back Out" as the final move. Example: logo-letters-eyes.
10. **Pop from the growth pivot** (speech bubbles, trays, pins): `set_pivot {id: <bubble>, at: [tail tip x, y]}`, `animate` scale 0 → 100 with ease: "spring Bouncy" 0.5 s; the second part 0.25 s later the same way.
11. **Literal hinge / open** (books, doors, envelopes, boxes, lids): `rig {kind: "hinge", ids: [<door>], t0: 0.3, duration: 0.6, options: {edge: "left", angle: 80, light: true}}` (angle ≤ 88); the content rises 40–80 px out of the opening 0.15 s after it is half open (`rig {kind: "reveal-from", options: {from: <door>, direction: "up"}}`). Pages fanning: `set_pivot` at the spine, `animate` rotation per page with ease: "Back Out", `choreograph {pattern: "sequential", step: 0.05}`. Example: logo-door-opens.
12. **Grow from the baseline** (plants, bars, rising type): `rig {kind: "grow", ids: [<parts>], options: {from: "bottom", stagger: 0.06}}`. Rising word: `split_text {by: "char"}`, each letter `animate` scale [100, 30] → [100, 110] → [100, 100] with ease: "spring Quick" (pivot at the baseline: `set_pivot {at: "bottom"}`), then `choreograph {pattern: "wave", spread: 0.3}`.
13. **Spin-open bloom** (radial, pinwheel, flower marks): petals with their pivot at the centre (`anchor: "bottom"` placed on the centre), each scale 0 → 100, ease: "Expo Out", 0.04 s apart with varied durations; the group pivots on the centre (`set_pivot {id: <group>, at: [cx, cy]}`) and turns −180 → +8 → 0 (Expo Out, then Sine In Out). Example: logo-spin-bloom.
14. **Parts land on the beat, then join**: parts pop with ease: "Back Out" on a 0.25–0.5 s grid (`align_to_beats {ids, mode: "sequence", bpm: 120}` or the music's beats), the joining piece arrives with `rig {kind: "swing"}`, then the whole group tilts 5–8° and settles (`animate` rotation 6 → 0, ease: "Back Out").
15. **Elastic stretch and snap** (playful marks and letters): `set_pivot` at the fixed end; `animate` scale [100, 100] → [260, 100] over 0.5 s with ease: "Sine In Out", back to [100, 100] with ease: "spring Wobbly" over 0.6 s, plus a −6° rotation kick at the release. Letters: `morph` to a stretched copy and `back: 0.1`.
16. **Giant crop pulls back** (bold, simple marks; confident, editorial): `rig {kind: "frame-detail", ids: [<mark>], t0: 0.25, duration: 1.2, options: {at: <a detail of the mark>, zoom: 6–9, tilt: 4}}` (Expo Out; `duration: 2.5, options: {ease: "Sine Out"}` for elegant); never from the dead centre; then recipe 2. Example: logo-giant-crop.
17. **Scale-through into the next beat**: `animate` scale 100 → 2500 with ease: "Expo In" over 0.5 s until the fill covers the frame (or `rig {kind: "frame-detail", mode: "out", options: {zoom: 25}}`), cut the background to the mark's colour on that frame (`rig {kind: "color-cuts", options: {colors: [<mark colour>], count: 1, settle: <mark colour>, ink: [<mark>]}}`), and the mark returns small with ease: "Expo Out" (spirals: +180° rotation).
18. **Letter slices lock in** (stencil, inline, modular type): `split_shape` (components) or `split_text {by: "char"}`; pieces from ±200 px on x, alternating, `animate` position with ease: "Expo Out" 0.35 s and `blur` 20 → 0 as a speed smear; `choreograph {pattern: "random", step: 0.03}`.
19. **Typewriter, the cursor becomes the mark**: `rig {kind: "type-on", ids: [<word>], options: {cursor: true, speed: 12}}` (≈ 0.08 s per character, hold keys); the result lists the created cursor: `morph {id: <cursor>, to: {shape: <mark shape> }, duration: 0.4, ease: "Expo Out"}` (or rotate it into the symbol).
20. **Old → new** (rebrands only): obsolete parts fade 0.3 s, the rest re-centres, each part morphs to its new role (`morph`, 0.8 s, ease: "Cubic In Out").
21. **Word cycle** (names that stand for many things): stacked text variants in one place, swapped with opacity hold keys every 0.7 s (`set_keyframes` with `interp: "hold"`), an underline whose width follows each word (`animate` size, 0.25 s, ease: "Cubic In Out"); land on the real name and hold.
22. **The mark as a window** (secondary layer, never the only idea): the mark as `mask: true` over image placeholders swapped every 0.5–0.7 s with hold keys; the solid fill returns over 0.3 s for the lockup.
23. **Background colour cuts on the beat** (on top of any recipe): `rig {kind: "color-cuts", options: {colors: [<brand palette>], every: 0.4–0.8, ink: [<word>, <mark>]}}` (or `bpm`); hold keys through the palette, settling on the primary; ink flips when the background's luminance does.
24. **Pulse ripple** (signal, health, audio): `duplicate_layers` the outline; the copy `animate` scale 100 → 180 and opacity 80 → 0 over 0.8 s with ease: "Sine Out", twice 0.4 s apart; the core `rig {kind: "beat"}`.
25. **Mirror exit / loopable**: after the hold, reverse the build at ~0.7× its duration with accelerating eases: the same rigs with `mode: "out"` (grow, unfold, assemble, reveal-from, type-on, draw-then-fill, frame-detail) in reverse order, or keys mirrored with ease: "Expo In"; end on the first frame so it loops (`loop {mode: "cycle", fitComp: true}` for keyed cycles). Example: logo-mirror-loop.

## Check (the critic's logo mode measures these)
A 12-frame `contact_sheet` of a good sting: frame 0 nearly empty; the mark recognisable only from frame ~4–5; the lockup complete by frame 5–6; ≥ 3 identical frames of the final lockup; you can name the verb of the idea ("assembles", "opens", "writes", "becomes"). `critique` flags: no lead-in, a late lock, a short hold, length vs pace, a wordmark that competes with the mark or fades in on top, moves that drift instead of snap, a big final move, mixed easing families, and marks whose parts only pop or fade.
